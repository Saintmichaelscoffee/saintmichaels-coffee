Keep your coffee close.

Add Saint Michaels Coffee to your home screen for quick access to our coffee, stories, and new releases. It’s the same website, in its own app window on supported devices. No app-store download or new account needed.

On iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then choose Add to Home Screen. You may need to scroll the share menu.
  3. If shown, keep Open as Web App switched on, then tap Add.

On Android

  1. Open this website in Chrome.
  2. Tap the Add to home screen button above if available, or open Chrome’s three-dot menu.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen, then confirm.

Menu wording varies by browser and device. If you opened this inside a social app, open it in Safari or Chrome first.

Already on your home screen?

Look for SMC Coffee. On a computer, supported browsers may also offer an install icon in the address bar.

You’ll need an internet connection for current products, prices, purchases, forms, and account access. Checkout is securely handled by Shopify and may open outside the app. The app doesn’t save checkout or account pages for offline use.

Looking for your affiliate tools? The Saint Michaels Coffee Portal is a separate app.
