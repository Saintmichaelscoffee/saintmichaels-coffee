Frequently Asked

How fresh is the coffee?

We roast in small batches and ship soon after roasting, so it reaches you near its peak instead of after months on a shelf.

Whole bean or ground?

Every roast comes either way. Pick what fits your setup at checkout.

How does the subscription work?

Choose a roast and a delivery rhythm. It ships automatically, you save on every order, and you can pause, swap, or cancel any time.

How does my purchase actually help?

A portion of every order funds the Saint Michaels Foundation — groceries, coats, a night in a motel, work boots, or a pair of gloves and a gym that stays open. Officers and firefighters on the ground decide where it goes.
