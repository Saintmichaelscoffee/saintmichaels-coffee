Frequently Asked

When does ordering open?

Online ordering is planned to open December 1, 2026, through the Saint Michaels Coffee Shopify store.

What coffee are you launching?

We are launching with one 12 oz Organic Espresso Blend. It can be prepared as espresso or brewed as a bold everyday coffee.

What will it cost?

A single bag will be $22.99. The planned subscription price is $20.99 per bag.

How does my purchase help?

Each bag supports the Saint Michaels Foundation mission of helping meet immediate community needs identified by officers, firefighters, and other people with boots on the ground.
