Before you inquire

Who can inquire about a coffee fundraiser?

Organizations supporting veterans, law enforcement, first responders, and local communities can tell us about their cause. Each inquiry is reviewed individually.

How are fundraising arrangements decided?

We discuss your organization’s goals and preferred timeframe, then confirm the details individually before a fundraiser begins.

Does submitting an inquiry launch a fundraiser?

No. An inquiry starts a conversation. Participation and program details are agreed individually before any fundraiser begins.
