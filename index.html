The person behind the purpose

One name.
A lifetime of service.

Our founder, Michael James Englert, is an Army veteran, law enforcement veteran, and boxing coach. The Saint Michael name began with Sister Mary Elaine, the woman who helped raise his father—and whose favorite saint inspired Michael’s name.

Saint Michaels Coffee carries that personal story forward through coffee, community, and a commitment to helping others.
