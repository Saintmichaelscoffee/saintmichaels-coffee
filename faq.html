Our coffee

Which coffees can I choose?

Answer the Call Organic Espresso and Stand Watch Single Origin Organic are available in 12 oz bags. Check the product options for current availability. Explore our coffee.

How do Answer the Call and Stand Watch differ?

Answer the Call has rich, dark-chocolate character. Stand Watch is a balanced medium roast from San Ignacio, Peru, with notes of milk chocolate, honey, and caramel. Compare the coffees.

Can I buy one bag of each coffee?

Yes. Coffee Duo pairs one 12 oz bag of Answer the Call with one 12 oz bag of Stand Watch. Review the current bundle price and eligibility at checkout. Shop Coffee Duo.

Are coffee pods available?

Coffee pods are in development and are not currently available to order. See current coffee options.

Orders and subscriptions

Where can I order Saint Michaels Coffee?

Choose your coffee on our website and continue to our Shopify storefront to select purchase options and complete checkout. Shop coffee.

Are subscriptions available?

Yes. Eligible coffee options offer subscriptions. Select the quantity and delivery frequency on the product page, then review the purchase terms and final price at checkout. Explore subscription options.

Are service discounts available?

Service discounts are available for eligible military, veterans, law enforcement, firefighters, EMS, and first responders. Use the service-discount page to review eligibility and apply. Discounts do not stack. View service discounts.

Where can I get help with shipping, returns, or an existing subscription?

Contact info@saintmichaelscoffee.com with your order question. Our support page contains the published shipping and returns information. Contact and support.

Community and purpose

Is the Saint Michaels Foundation operational?

No. The Saint Michaels Foundation is in development. It is not yet operational, accepting donations, or distributing assistance. Read the Foundation status.
