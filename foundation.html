Saint Michaels Foundation

COMING SOON

Help Should Not Have To Wait.

Saint Michaels Foundation is currently being built around a simple idea: when a frontliner encounters someone facing an immediate need, practical assistance should be within reach—that night, in that moment.

A safe room. Tonight. Our goal is to enable a first responder to see the need, make the call, and help move a family from uncertainty to safety.

The Mission

The People Who See It First

Officers, firefighters, EMTs, and other frontliners encounter needs that may never reach a traditional application or waiting list. They meet families without food, children without proper clothing, and people with nowhere safe to sleep.

The Foundation is being built to help those frontliners answer the need while they are still standing in front of it.

The Difference

Our goal: small, practical assistance. Delivered quickly. Guided by the people already on the ground.

How It Is Being Designed To Work

From Urgent Need To Immediate Help

01

A Frontliner Sees The Need

The Foundation is being designed for the moment a trusted responder encounters a person or family facing an immediate, practical emergency.

02

They Make The Call

Our goal is for the need to be communicated directly, without asking the family to navigate a long and complicated process.

03

Help Is Put Within Reach

The Foundation is being designed so available resources could be used for a safe room, food, clothing, transportation, or another urgent necessity.

Practical Assistance

What Immediate Help Can Look Like

The need may be small on paper and life-changing in the moment. These are the kinds of practical assistance we are building the Foundation to help provide.

  • Temporary lodging and safe shelter
  • Groceries and hot meals
  • Coats, shoes, and essential clothing
  • Towing, tires, and urgent transportation
  • Other immediate necessities identified on scene
