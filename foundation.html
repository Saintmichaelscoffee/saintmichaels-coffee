Saint Michaels Foundation

Help Should Not Have To Wait.

When a frontliner encounters someone facing an immediate need, Saint Michaels Foundation is being built to help put practical assistance within reach—that night, in that moment.

A safe room. Tonight. A first responder sees the need, makes the call, and helps move a family from uncertainty to safety.

The Mission

The People Who See It First

Officers, firefighters, EMTs, and other frontliners encounter needs that may never reach a traditional application or waiting list. They meet families without food, children without proper clothing, and people with nowhere safe to sleep.

The Foundation is designed to help those frontliners answer the need while they are still standing in front of it.

The Difference

Small, practical assistance. Delivered quickly. Guided by the people already on the ground.

How It Works

From Urgent Need To Immediate Help

01

A Frontliner Sees The Need

A trusted responder encounters a person or family facing an immediate, practical emergency.

02

They Make The Call

The need is communicated directly, without asking the family to navigate a long and complicated process.

03

Help Is Put Within Reach

Available resources can be used for a safe room, food, clothing, transportation, or another urgent necessity.

Practical Assistance

What Immediate Help Can Look Like

The need may be small on paper and life-changing in the moment.

  • Temporary lodging and safe shelter
  • Groceries and hot meals
  • Coats, shoes, and essential clothing
  • Towing, tires, and urgent transportation
  • Other immediate necessities identified on scene
